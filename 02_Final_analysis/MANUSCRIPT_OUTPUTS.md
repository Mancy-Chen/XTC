## Figures and separate captions

Figure annotations and axis labels are generated by `11_make_plots.py`. Statistical output identifiers such as `vwrec_delta` remain in machine-readable CSVs but are not used as Figure 1's axis label.

**Figure 1:** `plots/predefined_roi_voxelvolume/right_hippocampus_adjusted_vs_delta_vwrec.png`

Adjusted right hippocampal ROI volume change in relation to RAVLT delayed-recall change, stratified by XTC exposure group. Spearman coefficients are reported; lines and shaded bands show descriptive OLS fits and 95% confidence intervals.

The upper-left annotation lists XTC-naive above XTC users. The y-axis reads “Change in RAVLT delayed recall (words).” The caption above remains separate from the image; there is no embedded footnote.

**Figure S1:** `plots/bootstrap/whole_brain_PC1_top20_loading_bootstrap_intervals.png`

Bootstrap stability of the original top-20 whole-brain PC1 loadings. Points show original loadings and horizontal intervals show bootstrap percentile ranges.

**Figure S2A:** `plots/predefined_roi_pca/Combined_shape_only_top20_PC1_loadings.png`  
**Figure S2B:** `plots/predefined_roi_pca/Combined_shape_firstorder_top20_PC1_loadings.png`

Top absolute loadings from the predefined-ROI radiomics PCA. (A) PC1 loadings for the combined shape-only feature set. (B) PC1 loadings for the combined shape-plus-first-order feature set. LH, left hippocampus; RH, right hippocampus; LT, left thalamus; RT, right thalamus.

**Figure S3A:** `plots/whole_brain_pca/whole_brain_top20_PC1_loadings.png`

Top 20 absolute whole-brain PC1 loadings, with full anatomical region names. Positive loadings are blue; negative loadings are red.

**Figure S3B:** anatomical projections under `plots/whole_brain_pca/spatial_projection/`, generated by `12_spatial_loading_projection.py` only when an appropriate atlas is supplied.

Panel images are exported separately for assembly in the manuscript. Additional PC2–PC5 loading figures and scree plots are descriptive and do not introduce inferential tests of those components.
